Help

Questions, answered.

Is OLIVE FITNESS really women-only?

Yes — floor, batches and coaches are women-only. That privacy is the core of the brand.

What are the timings?

Mon–Sat: 6 AM–1 PM & 4–9 PM; Sunday: 6 AM–12 PM. (Tuesday afternoon hours aren't in public listings — confirm on call.)

How much is membership?

Prices aren't published online. See plan options (Monthly → Yearly), then call/WhatsApp +91 73582 94290 for the current price list.

How do I join or book a free trial?

Fill the contact/trial form or tap WhatsApp. We reply in gym hours. One free trial per person, no card needed.

I'm a complete beginner. Will I cope?

Yes. Free assessment, machine orientation and scaled workouts. Beginner batches available.

Do you offer personal training & diet help?

Yes — PT sessions in longer plans; diet guidance and progress reviews included per plan.

What should I wear / bring?

Comfortable workout wear, indoor shoes, towel, water bottle. Mats provided for yoga.

What about pause, transfer or cancellation?

Policy to be confirmed with the gym and published here before launch. Ask on WhatsApp for current terms.

Where are you located?

1st Floor, Apollo Building, 15, Kattabomman Main Road, 1st St, Kodungaiyur, Chennai 600118. Map and directions on the contact page.
