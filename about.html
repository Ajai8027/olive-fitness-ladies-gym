Our story

A gym where women belong from day one.

On Kattabomman Main Road, Kodungaiyur, OLIVE FITNESS is the neighbourhood's women-only answer to intimidating gyms: a clean, dust-free floor, modern equipment in excellent condition, coaches who teach form first, and morning + evening batches timed around college, work and home.

Our mission

Make strength, health and confidence accessible to every woman — beginner to athlete — in a safe, respectful space.

Our values

Privacy • Respect • Consistency over crash diets • Coaching with care • Cleanliness always.

What makes us different

Women-only floor, small batch sizes, certified women coaches, yoga-to-weights under one roof, progress tracking.

Why members choose Olive

✔ Beginner-safe onboarding

Free fitness assessment, machine orientation and a starter split so week one never feels lost.

✔ Real coaching, small batches

Trainers correct posture, scale workouts for PCOS, back pain, postpartum (with medical clearance) and more.

✔ Flexible for real life

Morning 6 AM–1 PM & evening 4–9 PM batches (Sun 6 AM–12 PM), easy enquiry on WhatsApp.

✔ Clean & comfortable

Sanitised equipment, changing room, lockers, drinking water — details that matter daily.
